This plan replaces generic assumptions in `GEO_LANDING_PAGE_PLAN.md` with the information supplied in `JOYCAFE_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`.

## 1. Agreed objective

Build a bilingual, static-first landing page that establishes **JoyCafe Hot&Cold** as a playful community café in Sidomulyo, Kota Batu, especially suitable for:

1. Groups of four to six people who want to gather or play board/card games.
2. Students and remote workers who need Wi-Fi, tables, and power outlets.
3. Visitors who want affordable coffee made with Indonesian beans and simple food/snacks.
4. Couples, families, and communities looking for a shared activity rather than only a place to sit.
5. Visitors near Hotel Purnama, Sidomulyo, Punten, or Bumiaji.

The primary business goal is awareness and reputation. The secondary goal is more in-store visits.

### Primary conversions

1. **Get Directions / Petunjuk Arah** — primary CTA.
2. **View Menu / Lihat Menu** — equal visual priority because menu fit drives visits.
3. **WhatsApp** — secondary CTA for questions and reservations.

### Success definition

The website succeeds when more qualified people discover JoyCafe, understand what it is good for, request directions or view the menu, and arrive with accurate expectations. AI mention/citation growth is a leading metric; increased café visits are the business outcome.

No implementation can guarantee an AI recommendation. The plan improves discoverability, clarity, corroboration, and recommendation fit, then measures changes over time.

## 3. Brand and visual direction

### Creative concept: “The Joy Table”

Design the experience around a shared tabletop: coffee rings, game tiles/cards, friendly rounded shapes, and generous group-table photography. It should feel joyful, playful, and calm—not industrial, sterile, glass-heavy, or aggressively minimal.

### Proposed palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Espresso | Deep brown | `#34231B` | Primary text and grounding color |
| Joy gold | Warm marigold | `#F2B632` | Brand highlight derived from the logo |
| Cream | Warm paper | `#FFF6E5` | Main background |

| Play | Soft coral | `#E96F51` | Game/activity accents and secondary CTA |
| Ink | Near-black | `#201C1A` | High-contrast body text |

Final contrast combinations must pass WCAG AA. Avoid placing normal-sized white text on joy gold.

### Typography direction

- Rounded, expressive display face for headlines and small game-like labels.
- Highly readable humanist sans-serif for body copy, facts, menu prices, and directions.
- Limit the site to two font families and use locally served or performance-safe files.

Potential starting pair: **Fredoka** for display and **Nunito Sans** for body. Confirm licensing and visual fit during implementation.

### Logo treatment

The supplied `image.png` contains a recognizable cup/person/coffee-bean mark and gold “JOY” lettering, but it is a flattened raster with small embedded address/social text and a 3D metallic effect.

Plan:

1. Use the existing asset only as a temporary reference, not as a large final hero logo.
2. Redraw the core mark and wordmark as clean vector artwork after the name/handle are confirmed.
3. Remove the address and Instagram handle from the logo; publish them as real HTML text.
4. Produce full-color, one-color, dark-background, favicon, and social-avatar variants.
5. Keep the recognizable smiling-cup idea while simplifying it for small screens.

Real café atmosphere photography is required for the hero. Do not replace it with generic or generated café imagery because the location itself is evidence for recommendation fit.
